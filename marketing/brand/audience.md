# الجمهور وما يجوز أن تقوله كور تيك

الأسبوع ليس موجهًا إلى «المهتمين بالتقنية». هو موجه إلى من يدير عملًا في السعودية ويقرأ بالعربية: صاحب منشأة، مؤسس شركة ناشئة، أو جهة طُلب منها تحوّل رقمي قبل أن تسمّي المشكلة. يتوقف إذا رأى الانقطاع الذي يعيشه، ثم جملةً موجودة أصلًا على الموقع. لا يتوقف لجملة طموح.

This week is brand-led. There is no performance file and no platform account to read. Nothing below is a result CTS has published.

## Who they are

They already run something. The owner of a small or mid-size company still closes the day across chats and sheets. A founder can tell the idea and cannot yet name the user, the scope, or a first version. An organization has been told to digitize and is about to buy a tool before anyone has stated the problem. A lead has been asked for “AI” as a slide, with no step in the work that would change.

Arabic is the language of the decision. English is how vendors talk at them. They have seen software arrive as a demo and miss the operation. They do not need a category education film. They need one precise sentence, then a next step that is already on the site: a direct technical conversation, the project kept confidential, on WhatsApp.

## Pains a film may name

These are the viewer’s situation. They are not case studies, not client stories, and not measurements.

1. The work moves, and the system does not. A request, a number, and an approval live apart.
2. An idea is being treated as a product before strategy, interface, and scope exist.
3. Someone asked for a build before the right question.
4. A screen presents the company and does not help a real user finish a real task.
5. Intelligence is a line in a deck, not a capability inside a product or a workflow.

Do not add a sector, a city, a revenue figure, or a before-and-after.
